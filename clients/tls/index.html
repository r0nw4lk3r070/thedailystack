// On the Radar

  • 30 Mar 2026
    Mistral AI raises €830M in debt to build a data centre near Paris, powered by Nvidia chips. CEO Arthur Mensch: “to ensure AI innovation and autonomy remain at the heart of Europe — rather than depend on third-party cloud providers.” 200 MW of European compute capacity by 2027.
    Source: TechCrunch  ·  Reuters  ·  CNBC
  • 31 Mar 2026
    LiteLLM supply chain attack via compromised PyPI account. Two malicious versions (1.82.7 & 1.82.8) active for 3 days, stealing SSH keys, cloud tokens, and Kubernetes secrets. If you’re building local AI on open-source: audit your dependency versions. Context →
    Source: BleepingComputer  ·  TeamPCP
  • 26 Mar 2026
    EU Parliament votes to simplify the AI Act. New deadlines for high-risk systems, a ban on nudifier apps, reduced overlap with sector legislation. What this means for your organisation →
    Source: European Parliament  ·  ronspoelstra.be
  • March 2026
    Axelera AI (Eindhoven) puts European AI chips on the map with the Metis APU — up to 629 TOPS inference on edge hardware. A serious European alternative to American dominance in AI inference.
