600 Years KU Leuven & 70 Years of Artificial Intelligence

Leuven.AI Conference
Monday 1 September – Tuesday 2 September 2025  ·  08:30 – 17:00 CEST  ·  KU Leuven University Hall (Promotiezaal / Jubileumzaal), Naamsestraat 22, 3000 Leuven  ·  €75–€600 (tiered: KU Leuven AI students €75–€150, Academics €150–€300, Others €300–€600)

About

KU Leuven turns 600 in 2025, and the field of artificial intelligence turns 70. This two-day conference marks both anniversaries with a programme that spans robotics, NLP, computer vision, AI hardware, philosophy, and EU regulation. For anyone working in or near Leuven’s AI ecosystem, this is the rare chance to hear researchers who normally keynote at NeurIPS and ICML speak in a lecture hall on Naamsestraat.

The conference is organised by Leuven.AI, KU Leuven’s interdisciplinary AI institute, which coordinates research groups including DTAI, PSI/MICAS, STADIUS, and LIRIS. Leuven.AI runs a regular seminar series and the annual Leuven.AI Conference. This 2025 edition is the flagship event of KU Leuven’s 600th anniversary year, hosted in the University Hall on Naamsestraat — the building at the centre of Leuven’s academic life since the 14th century.

Eight keynote speakers are confirmed: Pieter Abbeel (UC Berkeley) on humanoid robots, Marc Pollefeys (ETH Zürich / Microsoft) on spatial AI, Mark Coeckelbergh (University of Vienna) on AI and democracy, Sophie Vandebroek (Strategic Vision Ventures) on AI in industry, Walter Daelemans (University of Antwerp) on whether LLMs have solved NLP, Marian Verhelst (KU Leuven / imec) on escaping the hardware lottery, Guy Van den Broeck (UCLA) on symbolic reasoning and LLMs, and Lorelien Hoet (Microsoft) on the AI Act, GDPR, and DSA. The programme also includes science and industry exhibitions, poster sessions, and a public AI lecture on 31 August.

Speakers

Pieter Abbeel

Professor, Director of Robot Learning Lab — UC Berkeley

Professor at UC Berkeley, Director of the Robot Learning Lab, Co-Director of BAIR Lab, Co-Founder of covariant.ai and Gradescope, Advisor to OpenAI. His research focuses on apprenticeship learning, reinforcement learning, and meta-learning for robotics.

Talk: The Upcoming Era of Humanoid Robots

Reinforcement Learning Humanoid Robotics Robot Learning Meta-Learning

Marc Pollefeys

Professor of Computer Science, Director of Microsoft Spatial AI Lab — ETH Zürich / Microsoft

Professor at ETH Zürich and Director of Microsoft Spatial AI Lab. Pioneer in 3D computer vision — first to develop an automatic photo-to-3D pipeline. Fellow of IEEE, ACM, AAIA, and ELLIS. KU Leuven PhD alumnus (1999).

Talk: Spatial AI

3D Vision Spatial Intelligence Computer Vision 3D Reconstruction

Mark Coeckelbergh

Professor of Philosophy — University of Vienna

Philosopher of technology and AI ethics expert. ERA Chair at the Czech Academy of Sciences, member of the EU High Level Expert Group on AI. Author of ‘Why AI Undermines Democracy And What To Do About It’.

Talk: AI and the Future of Democracy

AI Ethics Philosophy of AI Democracy EU AI Policy

Sophie Vandebroek

Founder — Strategic Vision Ventures LLC

Seasoned executive with C-level experience at IBM, Xerox, and UTC. Expert in creating and applying technologies that drive growth. Has served on public and private company boards since 2008.

Talk: AI in Industry (on-stage interview)

AI in Industry Technology Strategy Corporate Innovation Executive Leadership

Walter Daelemans

Professor of Computational Linguistics — University of Antwerp

Directs the CLiPS research group at the University of Antwerp. Research spans machine learning for NLP, computational psycholinguistics, computational stylometry, and language technology applications.

Talk: Have Large Language Models solved Natural Language Processing?

NLP Large Language Models Computational Linguistics Stylometry

Marian Verhelst

Professor, Research Director at imec — KU Leuven / imec

Professor at MICAS labs, KU Leuven, and imec research director. Research in embedded ML, hardware accelerators, and HW-algorithm co-design. IEEE Fellow, holder of 2 ERC grants, Intel Outstanding Researcher Award 2021.

Talk: AI Needs New Engines: Escaping the Hardware Lottery

AI Hardware Embedded ML HW/SW Co-Design Hardware Accelerators

Guy Van den Broeck

Professor of Computer Science, Samueli Fellow — UCLA

Directs the StarAI lab at UCLA. Research in tractable deep generative models, statistical relational learning, and probabilistic inference. His work shows how symbolic reasoning algorithms integrated into LLMs enable state-of-the-art controllable text generation and alignment.

Talk: Symbolic Reasoning in the Age of Large Language Models

Symbolic Reasoning Neurosymbolic AI Probabilistic Inference LLM Alignment

Lorelien Hoet

Director of EU Government Affairs — Microsoft

Director of EU Government Affairs at Microsoft, working on EU data protection, law enforcement, and online safety policy. Former legal executive at Proximus and Director of Regulatory Affairs at Mobistar/Orange. Holds LL.Ms from KU Leuven and ULB.

Talk: Touchpoints, overlaps and possible synergies between the AI Act, the GDPR and the DSA

EU AI Act GDPR Digital Services Act EU Regulation
