About

AICD Brussels is a free, full-day community conference at Microsoft Belgium in Zaventem covering keynotes, hands-on workshops, and panel discussions on AI tools, frameworks, ethics, and governance. Part of a global series spanning 50+ cities — San Francisco, Tokyo, London, Paris, Frankfurt, Dubai — the Brussels edition brings international-quality AI content to the local Belgian tech community without requiring a flight out of the country.

The format is community-driven, not corporate. Events are organised by local AI professionals, developers, and enthusiasts, with global sponsorship from Microsoft and the Microsoft Global Community Initiative (MGCI). Past AICD editions have covered Generative AI, ML ops, Azure AI, responsible AI, and practical implementation patterns. The Brussels edition is expected to follow the same mix: real practitioners sharing real experience, not vendor keynotes.

AICD Brussels is the most accessible free AI conference in Belgium this spring, held right at Zaventem — an easy commute from Leuven. It covers exactly the intersection of AI, developer tooling, and community that TLS readers care about. Speakers at AICD events tend to be engineers and researchers who have actually shipped AI-powered systems, which makes the workshops and panel discussions more grounded than the average conference circuit.

What AICD Talks Are Usually About

Generative AI and Large Language Models in Practice

AICD events consistently feature sessions on putting LLMs to work in real systems — prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation, fine-tuning for domain-specific tasks, and evaluating model behaviour in production. Speakers tend to be engineers who have shipped LLM-powered features and can speak to the gap between demo performance and what actually runs reliably at scale. Brussels editions have previously covered Azure OpenAI integrations, Copilot extensions, and open-source model deployments.

Responsible AI: Ethics, Governance, and Compliance

Ethics and governance are structural parts of every AICD programme, not afterthoughts. The community places particular emphasis on the EU AI Act, bias detection in production models, explainability requirements, and building governance frameworks that legal, product, and engineering teams can actually operate together. With Belgium inside the EU regulatory perimeter, sessions on compliance with the AI Act tend to attract a lot of engagement from enterprise and public sector attendees.

Azure AI and Cloud-Native AI Solutions

Given the Microsoft Global Community Initiative sponsorship and the Brussels venue at Microsoft Belgium, Azure AI services feature prominently at AICD events — Azure Machine Learning, Azure AI Studio, Cognitive Services, and how these integrate with enterprise Microsoft stacks. Sessions typically cover architecture patterns for cloud-native AI deployments, MLOps on Azure, and cost/performance trade-offs of managed vs self-hosted models. This is not exclusively Microsoft content — multi-cloud and open-source patterns also appear regularly.

Community AI Projects and Local Case Studies

One of AICD’s signature elements is the community showcase format, where local practitioners present projects they have actually built. In the Brussels context, this tends to surface AI applications from Belgian startups, corporate innovation teams, and university research groups. Past showcases have covered everything from NLP tools for Dutch and French text processing to computer vision applications in logistics and manufacturing. The format is deliberately peer-to-peer rather than sponsor-driven, which produces more candid discussions about what works and what does not.
