Sources & References

All sources used across the sector articles on this site, in one place.

These references are cited in the sector articles under For Business. Each reference has a permanent ID — for example #ref-1 — so future articles can link directly to a source. References are organised by category.

EU Legislation

  1. GDPR — Regulation (EU) 2016/679 General Data Protection Regulation — the primary data protection law for all personal data processing in the EU/EEA. Includes Art. 6 (legal basis), Art. 7 (consent), Art. 9 (special categories), Art. 22 (automated decision-making). eur-lex.europa.eu
  2. AI Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 EU Artificial Intelligence Act — risk classification framework for AI systems. Annex III lists high-risk AI applications including employment screening, biometric identification, and systems used in critical services. eur-lex.europa.eu
  3. NIS2 — Directive (EU) 2022/2555 Network and Information Security Directive 2 — cybersecurity obligations for essential and important entities. Applies to sectors including transport, health, digital infrastructure, and their supply chains. eur-lex.europa.eu
  4. DORA — Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 Digital Operational Resilience Act — ICT risk management and third-party oversight for financial entities. Requires financial institutions to audit ICT suppliers in their chain, including accountants and legal advisors who process financial data. eur-lex.europa.eu
  5. AMLD5 — Directive (EU) 2018/843 Fifth Anti-Money Laundering Directive — extends AML obligations to lawyers, accountants, and estate agents. Requires due diligence, transaction monitoring, and suspicious activity reporting. eur-lex.europa.eu
  6. ePrivacy Directive — 2002/58/EC Electronic Privacy Directive — governs confidentiality of electronic communications, cookie consent, and use of tracking technologies. Currently still applicable alongside the GDPR. eur-lex.europa.eu
  7. IDD — Insurance Distribution Directive (EU) 2016/97 Insurance Distribution Directive — governs the sale and distribution of insurance products. Sets conduct, disclosure, and product oversight requirements for insurance intermediaries and brokers. eur-lex.europa.eu
  8. EU Regulation 561/2006 — Driving times and rest periods Regulation on harmonisation of social legislation relating to road transport. Sets maximum driving times, mandatory rest periods, and tachograph requirements for professional drivers. eur-lex.europa.eu

Belgian Law

  1. Art. 458 Belgian Penal Code — Professional secrecy The core Belgian provision on professional secrecy. Applies to doctors, lawyers, psychologists, social workers, and all professions bound by confidentiality. Violation is a criminal offence. ejustice.just.fgov.be
  2. ITAA Act 17 March 2019, Art. 58 — Professional secrecy for accountants Act on the professions of accountant and tax adviser. Article 58 imposes statutory professional secrecy on all ITAA members — legally enforceable, not merely ethical. economie.fgov.be
  3. Act of 10 May 2007 combating discrimination — Belgium Belgian anti-discrimination law prohibiting bias based on age, gender, origin, disability, and other protected characteristics. Relevant for AI used in recruitment and employment decisions. ejustice.just.fgov.be
  4. LIMOSA — Registration of foreign workers (Belgium) Belgian system for registering foreign workers temporarily deployed in Belgium. Mandatory for construction and other sectors with cross-border labour. limosa.be
  5. Code of Economic Law, Art. III.86 — Accounting retention obligation (Belgium) Belgian statutory obligation to retain accounting documents for 7 years. Creates tension with the GDPR storage limitation principle — the legal retention obligation takes precedence. economie.fgov.be

Dutch Law

  1. WGBO — Art. 7:457 BW — Medical Treatment Contracts Act Dutch Civil Code provision on medical confidentiality. Prohibits healthcare providers from disclosing patient information without explicit consent. Basis for data protection in Dutch medical and mental health practice. wetten.overheid.nl
  2. BIG-wet — Art. 88 — Individual Healthcare Professions Act Dutch law governing healthcare professions (BIG register). Article 88 imposes a statutory duty of confidentiality on all registered healthcare professionals. wetten.overheid.nl
  3. Wkba — Chain Liability Act (Netherlands) Dutch Wet ketenaansprakelijkheid — makes main contractors jointly liable for unpaid wages and taxes of subcontractors. Especially relevant in construction and transport. wetten.overheid.nl
  4. AWR Art. 52 — General Tax Act, retention obligation (Netherlands) Dutch statutory obligation to retain financial and administrative records for 7 years. Analogous to Belgian Art. III.86 — creates the same tension with GDPR storage limitation. wetten.overheid.nl

Belgian Regulators

  1. GBA — Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit (Data Protection Authority Belgium) Belgian Data Protection Authority — supervises GDPR compliance, issues decisions, and handles complaints. Notably ruled on the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF) in 2022. gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be
  2. FSMA — Financial Services and Markets Authority (Belgium) Belgian financial sector supervisor — oversees insurance intermediaries, investment firms, and compliance with conduct rules including IDD and MiFID II. fsma.be
  3. eHealth platform Belgium Federal platform for the secure digital exchange of health data in Belgium. Governs which healthcare actors may exchange patient data and under what conditions. ehealth.fgov.be
  4. Unia — Interfederal Centre for Equal Opportunities (Belgium) Belgian anti-discrimination body. Relevant for AI in employment and recruitment: Unia monitors and litigates discriminatory automated decisions. unia.be

Dutch Regulators

  1. AP — Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Netherlands) Dutch Data Protection Authority — supervises GDPR compliance in the Netherlands, issues guidance and enforcement decisions. autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl
  2. AFM — Authority for the Financial Markets (Netherlands) Dutch financial markets supervisor — oversees banks, insurers, investment firms, and financial advisors. Enforces conduct rules including requirements relevant to AI use in financial advice. afm.nl
  3. College voor de Rechten van de Mens (Netherlands) Dutch Institute for Human Rights — handles discrimination complaints outside the courts, including cases involving algorithmic or AI-driven discrimination. mensenrechten.nl

Belgian Professional Bodies

  1. ITAA — Institute of Accountants and Tax Consultants (Belgium) Statutory professional body for accountants and tax advisers in Belgium. Sets professional ethics, continuing education requirements, and professional secrecy obligations. itaa.be
  2. OVB — Orde van Vlaamse Balies — Codex Deontologie Bar of Flemish Lawyers — the deontological code governs professional confidentiality, conflict of interest, and independence for lawyers in Belgium. ordevanvlaamsebalies.be
  3. VVKP — Flemish Association of Clinical Psychologists Belgian professional association for clinical psychologists. Maintains professional ethics standards including confidentiality rules that interact with GDPR Art. 9 (health data). vvkp.be
  4. Federgon — Federation of HR service providers (Belgium) Belgian federation of temporary staffing, search, selection, and outplacement firms. Relevant for AI in recruitment: sector guidance on automated screening. federgon.be
  5. Federatie Horeca Vlaanderen Flemish hospitality sector federation. Represents cafes, restaurants, hotels, and provides guidance on labour law (joint committee PC 302) and sector compliance. horeca.be
  6. Coiffure EU — National Association of Hairdressers Belgium Belgian professional association for the hair and beauty sector. Manages collective labour agreement PC 314 and sector-specific regulations. coiffure.be
  7. FEBETRANS — Federation of Belgian Road Transport Belgian road haulage federation. Publishes guidance on tachograph obligations, driving time regulations, and AI use in fleet management. febetrans.be
  8. Assuralia — Professional Association of Belgian Insurers Belgian insurance industry body. Represents insurers and publishes sector guidance on data protection, AI in underwriting, and FSMA compliance. assuralia.be
  9. Confederatie Bouw — Belgian Construction Federation Belgian construction sector federation. Provides guidance on LIMOSA obligations, subcontracting chain liability, and labour law for the building industry. confederatiebouw.be
  10. UNIZO — Union of Self-Employed Entrepreneurs (Belgium) Belgian organisation for self-employed professionals and SMEs. Publishes practical guidance on digital tools, AI, and compliance for small businesses. unizo.be

Dutch Professional Bodies

  1. NBA — Royal Dutch Institute of Chartered Accountants Dutch professional body for accountants. Publishes VGBA (conduct and professional rules), professional ethics guidance, and standards for accounting practice. nba.nl
  2. NOvA — Nederlandse Orde van Advocaten Dutch Bar Association. Sets conduct rules (Gedragsregels voor de Advocatuur) governing confidentiality, independence, and conflict of interest for Dutch lawyers. advocatenorde.nl
  3. KHN — Royal Dutch Hospitality Association (Koninklijke Horeca Nederland) Dutch hospitality sector organisation. Represents hotels, restaurants, and cafes — publishes sector guidance on labour law, data processing, and compliance. khn.nl
  4. INretail — Dutch Retail Association Dutch non-food retail sector federation. Publishes annual sector figures and guidance on digital tools, customer data, and e-commerce compliance. inretail.nl
  5. ANKO — Algemene Nederlandse Kappers Organisatie Dutch national hairdressers organisation. Manages sector labour agreement and practical guidance on privacy and client data for hair and beauty professionals. anko.nl
  6. TLN — Transport en Logistiek Nederland Dutch transport and logistics sector organisation. Publishes practical guidance on tachograph compliance, driver data, and AI in fleet management. tln.nl
  7. ABU — Algemene Bond Uitzend-ondernemingen Dutch general temporary staffing association. Sets collective labour agreements and guidance on data processing in recruitment and placement. abu.nl
  8. NBBU — Dutch Association of Flexible Employment Agencies Dutch flexible workforce industry association — alongside ABU, sets norms for temporary staffing, including responsible use of AI screening tools. nbbu.nl
  9. Verbond van Verzekeraars (Netherlands) Dutch Insurers Association — publishes sector guidance on digital transformation, AI in underwriting, and data protection for the Dutch insurance industry. verzekeraars.nl
  10. Bouwend Nederland Dutch construction sector federation. Publishes guidance on subcontracting liability (Wkba), labour law compliance, and digital tools in the building industry. bouwendnederland.nl
  11. MKB-Nederland Dutch SME federation — represents small and medium-sized businesses across all sectors. Publishes practical guidance on AI, digital tools, and compliance for SMEs. mkb.nl

Sector & Other

  1. Meta Pixel — operation and data collection Meta Business documentation on how the Facebook/Meta Pixel tracks user behaviour and transmits data to Meta servers. Relevant for retail and e-commerce GDPR compliance analysis. facebook.com/business
  2. Comeos — Retail in Belgium (sector facts) Comeos is the Belgian federation for retail and commerce. Publishes annual sector figures including number of businesses, turnover, and employment in Belgian retail. comeos.be
  3. GBA — IAB Europe TCF decisions (2022) — cookie consent Belgian DPA decisions on the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework. Found that real-time bidding consent strings constitute personal data and that the TCF as implemented violated the GDPR. gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be
  4. PC 302 — Joint Labour Committee for the hotel industry (Belgium) Paritair Comité 302 — sets the collective labour agreement for Belgian hotels, restaurants, and cafes. Governs wages, working hours, and conditions for hospitality workers. horeca.be
